Kit Assistant - Privacy Policy

Kit Assistant is a private, personal app. It is used only by its owner to read and organize their own Google account data (such as email and calendar) for personal household tasks.

  • It is not offered to the public, and no one else's data is collected.
  • Data accessed through Google APIs is used only to perform tasks the owner asks for.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
